# Foot and ankle: cited numbers for building and checking a 3-D foot by measurement

Compiled 2026-10-19 from abstracts, open full texts (Europe PMC / PMC) and page text. Nothing saved for use. Clinical, anthropometric, biomechanical sources; adults unless stated.
Confidence: strong = several studies agree or one very large sample; single = one study; weak = n<20, abstract only, secondary, preprint, handbook table with no named source, or recalled from memory. "derived" = my arithmetic on cited numbers. "via X" = read in X, not the primary. FL = foot length, WB = weight-bearing, BW = body weight (ball width is always spelled out), HW = heel width, M/F = male/female, MT/PP/MP/DP = metatarsal / proximal / middle / distal phalanx.

## 0. In my words: structure, behaviour, checks, method
(1) Structure. Three bone blocks (hindfoot talus+calcaneus ~60 and ~80 mm long; midfoot; forefoot of 5 metatarsals + 14 phalanges) under a sole that is skin 1.4-2.9 mm over a two-layer fat pad: a thin microchamber layer against the skin (~3-4 mm at the heel, barely compressible) and a thick macrochamber layer over bone (13 mm at the heel, 5-6 mm under the metatarsal heads, does nearly all the compressing). The dorsum is skin directly over tendons, veins and bone. The ankle is a near-fixed hinge (talocrural, axis ~82 deg to the tibial axis, through the malleoli) stacked on a variable oblique subtalar axis (~42 deg up from horizontal, 16-23 deg medial of the foot line) with hinges at the MTP joints. The lateral malleolus is lower (~13 mm) and further back than the medial.
(2) Behaviour. Standing is mostly a lowering of the instep and a spreading of the forefoot, finished by about half BW per foot (half vs 80% BW differ <0.2%): length +1-1.5% (~3 mm), forefoot/ball width +9-10% (~8-9 mm), instep height -14 to -16% (~10 mm), navicular -7 to -10 mm, heel pad roughly halves (16.9 -> 8.2 mm), plantar contact area +11% forefoot, +17% midfoot, +23% rearfoot. Men's feet are bigger; as a fraction of FL the sex differences are small (0.3-0.6% FL), and arch height index and rigidity show no sex difference. Age thickens the heel pad (17.6 -> 20.1 mm young vs elderly); higher BMI gives a thicker pad.
(3) Descriptors to check (target; spread; section):
 D1 FL: M 245-266, F 225-240 mm, SD 9-14 (sample dependent; F/M ratio 0.91-0.92) s1
 D2 ball width/FL 0.41-0.42; heel width/FL 0.255-0.27; instep (50% FL) height/FL 0.24-0.26 s1
 D3 truncated FL (heel to MTP1 centre)/FL 0.73-0.74; heel to MTP5/FL 0.655 s1
 D4 arch height index standing 0.31-0.34 (SD 0.03), sitting 0.36-0.37; rigidity 0.91-0.92 s2
 D5 arch index (middle third / footprint without toes) 0.23 +-0.04 young; <0.21 high, >0.26 low s2
 D6 navicular height standing 36-42 mm; navicular drop 6.4 mm static, 5.3 mm walking s2-3
 D7 weight-bearing change: FL +3, ball width +9, instep -10 mm (s3)
 D8 heel pad unloaded 17 mm (SD 2), loaded at 80% BW 8 mm; skin 1.7 mm (heel 2.2) s4
 D9 metatarsal head order M2>M1 by ~2 mm, M3 -4 mm, M4 -9 mm, M5 -14 mm from M2 (along foot axis) s5
 D10 ray lengths (bone): hallux ray 122 mm, 2nd ray 136 mm; M1 62-67 mm s5
 D11 lateral malleolus tip ~13 mm below medial tip; bimalleolar width 63-70 mm; ankle axis tilt ~8 deg s6-8
 D12 Achilles at malleolar level: thickness 4.5-5.4 mm, area 52-61 mm2 (derived width 12-16 mm) s8
(4) Measuring on a mesh. Foot axis: heel point (pternion) to the second-toe tip, floor plane at z=0. FL = bounding length along the axis. Widths: cross-section at 15% FL (heel), ball width between MTP1 and MTP5 heads (65-80%/50-80% FL, Volumental rule), instep height = max z in the 50% FL section. Footprint = z<1 mm contact patch; arch index = area of the middle third (excl. toes) / total. Navicular height = z of the tuberosity (most medial dorsal bump). Heel pad = skin-to-calcaneus distance in a sagittal section at the heel centre. Malleoli = local maxima of lateral/medial protrusion near z = 0.039 H; report height difference, AP offset, width. Achilles = sagittal and transverse sections at malleolar level and +15 mm. Nail curvature = transverse section at mid-plate (arc over chord). Report each as value +- tolerance against the rows below.
