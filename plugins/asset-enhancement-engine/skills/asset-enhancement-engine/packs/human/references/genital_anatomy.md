# External genital and perineal anatomy of adults (clinical, descriptive): cited numbers for mesh checks

Compiled 2026-10-19 from abstracts, open full texts (Europe PMC) and one author-manuscript PDF (text extracted locally). Clinical and anthropometric sources only; adults only; nothing downloaded for use.
Confidence: strong = 2+ independent sources agree; single = one study; weak = abstract/snippet, secondary summary or unverified. "Derived" = my arithmetic from a cited number. Rn = reference list at the end.
Limit: the web-search budget ran out part-way. Skin colour, male glans, posture effects, pubic-hair density and the Tanner-5 wording are therefore thin; "not found" there means not retrieved, not proven absent.

## 0. Build, behaviour, descriptors, mesh method (own words)
Female. From the front of the pelvis backward: mons pubis (fat pad over the pubic symphysis, hair-bearing keratinised skin); two labia majora (hair-bearing outer skin, fatty, closing the cleft in standing); two labia minora (hairless thin folds inside, free edge, very variable); the vestibule (non-keratinised mucosa) holding the urethral meatus and the vaginal introitus; the clitoral glans under its hood (prepuce) at the anterior join of the minora; the posterior fourchette; the perineal body between fourchette and anus. Most of the clitoris is internal (body, paired crura, bulbs); only the glans (about 6 x 5 mm) and hood show. Male. Penis (shaft, glans with corona, foreskin if present), scrotum (skin + dartos muscle around two testes; the left was the smaller in R20), and the perineum from scrotum to anus.
Behaviour. Female dimensions differ 2-5 fold between normal women (labia minora length 5-100 mm) with no significant left-right difference; labia minora, clitoral size, clitoris-urethra distance and perineum shorten with age, labia majora and introitus lengthen with BMI and vaginal birth (section 2). Penis: stretched length exceeds flaccid length by about 4 cm (derived), so "flaccid" must be defined by how it is measured. Testis volume is about 17 mL at age 35, right larger than left. Temperature and dartos tone change scrotal and penile form (qualitative, no cited number).
Measurable descriptors a mesh can be checked against (target, spread; rows detailed in sections below):
- F1 labia minora length 42 mm (SD 16, range 5-100; 5th-95th 25-70 at 15-24 y); definition-dependent, other studies 35-61 mm. F2 labia minora width 13-14 mm (SD 8; other studies 15-22). F3 length:width about 3 (derived).
- F4 labia majora length 80 mm (SD 15). F5 clitoral glans 6.4-6.9 mm long x 4.6-5.1 mm wide (two sources agree). F6 glans-to-meatus 22.3-22.6 mm. F7 introitus length 28 mm (SD 10). F8 perineum (fourchette to anterior anal margin) 21 mm (SD 8.5); fourchette to anal centre 38 mm (SD 6). F9 clitoris-to-anus 76-80 mm.
- F10 skin: epidermis 0.08 (minora) to 0.21 mm (majora), dermis 1.8-2.2 mm (50 MHz ultrasound); a lower-frequency probe gave dermis 0.8 mm.
- M1 flaccid pendulous penile length 9.2 cm (SD 1.6); M2 stretched 13.2 cm (SD 1.9); M3 flaccid circumference 9.3 cm (SD 0.9). M4 testis volume 17.9 mL right, 16.5 mL left (SD 4.1-4.4). M5 testis L:W:H about 1.55 : 1 : 0.75. M6 anus to posterior scrotum 45 mm (fertile men).
- H1 Tanner-5 pubic hair: inverted-triangle field, reaching medial thighs, not up the linea alba (secondary sources). Density and shaft numbers: not found.
How to measure on the mesh. Female: pose the mesh in the same posture as the data (lithotomy, thighs abducted, labia unstretched); landmarks = glans apex, glans base/hood edge, meatus centre, posterior fourchette, anterior anal margin, anal centre. Take distances along the midline sagittal section (surface path), labia minora length/width as geodesic from the clitoral hood to the lower margin and from the interlabial sulcus to the free edge. Closure check (derived, definitions from Figure 1 of R1 not read): glans 6.9 + glans-to-meatus 22.6 + introitus 27.9 + perineum 21.3 = 78.7 mm against labia majora length 80 mm. Male: dorsal pubopenile junction to meatus (pendulous), circumference at mid-shaft and at the corona, testis volume from the enclosed mesh volume (or L x W x H x 0.52) after removing skin and epididymis, anus-to-scrotum distance. Equivalent radius of the glans about 2.3-2.6 mm (derived from width 4.6-5.1 mm). Judge a value inside its 5th-95th range where given, not against the mean.

## 7. Posture
- Every vulval measurement above was taken in lithotomy (R1-R8, R11; R4 under general anaesthesia). No study gave standing or sitting vulval dimensions. In standing with thighs adducted the labia majora close the cleft (qualitative, derived reasoning, not a cited measurement): do not test a standing mesh against the lithotomy numbers without stating the posture.
- Straining raises genital hiatus and perineal body (R15); maximum prolapse is best seen standing (R15). No numbers retrieved.
- Penis and scrotum: effect of standing, sitting, cold, warmth on length, girth and scrotal depth: not found.

## 8. Not found
- Female: pigment/colour of labia minora, majora, vestibule (colorimetry, L*a*b*); labia minora free-edge thickness; hymen and vestibule dimensions; mons and majora fat-pad thickness; POP-Q genital hiatus and perineal body norms; standing/sitting shape.
- Male: glans diameter, length and corona circumference; foreskin length by circumcision status; scrotal skin thickness and its change with dartos tone; testis length/width/height measured directly (only derived); scrotal depth; Tanner-5 numbers for testis and penis; penile skin thickness.
- Hair: pubic follicle density, shaft diameter, and extent in cm; male extension up the linea alba (stage 6 of Reynolds and Wines) and its prevalence.
- Both: skin and mucosa colour differences (instrument values).
