# Human eye and surrounds (adult, relaxed, primary gaze): cited numbers for mesh checks

Compiled 2026-10-19 from abstracts and open pages (Europe PMC records, PMC, publisher abstracts, a few Wikipedia pages). Nothing downloaded for use; no files kept. The web-search quota ran out midway, so some rows come from search-result summaries (weak), and several full texts were unreachable (paywall, captcha).
Confidence: strong = 3+ independent sources agree; single = one study; weak = abstract/snippet only, textbook value with no reachable source, or second-hand. "Derived" = my arithmetic from cited numbers. Rn = reference list at the end.
Hard truth first: I could not reach any measured value for iris crypt/collarette relief depth, sphincter/dilator width, sclera scattering coefficients, lash curl angle, lid-margin thickness, or MRD1/MRD2 norms. Those rows are textbook, derived, or in the "not found" list.

## 0. Summary, how to use the tables, how to measure
(1) Build and behaviour. The sclera is a near-sphere shell (outer R about 12.0 mm; 1.0 mm thick at the back, 0.4-0.5 at the equator, 0.5 at the limbus). A smaller, steeper, aspheric clear cornea (R 7.8 front, 6.5 back, 11.8 mm wide, 0.53-0.55 mm thick at centre, thicker toward the edge, flatter toward the edge) is set into its front and bulges about 1 mm beyond the sclera's own sphere; the two slopes meet at the limbus as a shallow groove (kink 16-20 deg, derived) and the limbus is wider than tall (11.5 x 10.6 mm). Behind it: aqueous (about 3 mm), the iris, a pigmented diaphragm 0.3-0.6 mm thick and about 12 mm across at roughly 3.6 mm depth, and the lens (about 4 mm thick, 10 mm wide). The iris is layered: front border layer and stroma (melanocytes; colour comes from the amount of melanin in a fixed number of cells, not from cell number), a sphincter ring at the pupil margin and a radial dilator behind, and a two-cell black pigment epithelium on the back. The collarette ridge (about 1.5 mm out from the pupil edge) is its thickest line and splits a smooth pupillary zone from a ciliary zone with crypts and contraction furrows. The pupil runs 2-8 mm, smaller with age and light, centre slightly off the limbus centre. A tear film about 3 um thick (watery-mucin layer about 3.3 um, lipid about 50 nm on top) covers the front, with a concave meniscus (radius about 0.37 mm) along each lid margin. The fissure is about 28-31 mm wide and 10-11 mm tall in primary gaze; the lateral canthus sits a few degrees above the medial. Lashes of about 9-10 mm (1/3 of eye width, derived H6) fringe the margins.
Behaviour: the globe turns about a point roughly 13.5 mm behind the cornea (textbook, weak); the upper lid follows vertical gaze almost one to one (lid saccades lag the eye by about 5 ms), losing only about 1 mm of margin-to-reflex distance in downgaze; a blink closes in about 76 ms and reopens in 400+ ms. With age: pupil smaller, lens thicker, chamber shallower, fissure +3 mm from 12 to 25 y then -2.5 mm to old age. Sex: men slightly larger (axial +0.6 mm, corneal radius +0.15 mm, fissure +0.7 mm, IPD +2-3 mm).
(2) Descriptors: every table row below is a checkable target (ID, value with spread, n/population/method, source, confidence). Highest-value checks: A2, A6, A9, B1, B3, B4, B7, B8, B10, C1, C4, C9, D1, E1, H1, H2, H6.
(3) Measuring on a mesh. Landmarks: corneal apex (max z along the visual axis), limbus loop (where surface slope jumps), pupil-margin loop, collarette loop, medial/lateral canthus, lid-margin points over pupil centre, globe centre (best-fit sphere to the scleral patch). Fit: sphere to sclera (A2: R 11.5-12.5); conic z(r) = (r^2/R)/(1+sqrt(1-(1+Q)r^2/R^2)) to the cornea for r < 5 mm (B1, B3); read sag at r = 5.9 mm (B7) and the slope step across the limbus (B8). Section the front shell along the normal for thickness (B6, back R 6.5). Limbus height/width ratio from the loop (B4: 0.90-0.93). Pupil/iris: plane depth from apex (C1), pupil diameter, collarette radius = pupil radius + 1.5 mm, thickness by ray from front to back surface. Lids: fissure width/height between canthi and margins at pupil centre (H1-H3), canthal tilt, lid margins tangent to the globe with no gap, cover of the limbus by each lid, lash length and count. Rendered pupils/irises are apparent (cornea-magnified, see G4); compare with measured photo values only through that factor.
