# The mouth (lips, teeth, gingiva, tongue, palate, opening, wetness): cited numbers for mesh checks

Compiled 2026-10-19 from abstracts, open full texts (Europe PMC full-text API, publisher HTML, PDFs read as text). The shared web-search budget ran out part-way, so some sub-topics have no verified number (section 11). Lips seen from outside (Farkas) are in `head_face.md` s.7; tissue stiffness in `soft_tissue_mechanics.md`; both not repeated.
Confidence: strong = 3+ independent studies agree; single = one study; weak = abstract/snippet only, second-hand (review, encyclopaedia, textbook quoted by another paper) or unverified. "Derived" = my arithmetic. Rn = reference list at the end. Spread in brackets = SD unless stated.

## A. How it is built, how it behaves
- Lips: skin lip + red lip (vermilion) meeting at the vermilion border, over orbicularis oris; the philtrum runs from subnasale (sn) to labiale superius (ls); the commissures (cheilion, ch) close the mouth. In the 3-D adult data the lower vermilion is 1.3-1.4 x the upper (derived), width differs by sex (men ~3 mm wider), and with age the red lip thins (upper 8.1 -> 6.6 mm from the 20s to 50s) while the skin lip lengthens (R4).
- Teeth: enamel crown (up to ~2.5 mm thick at cusps, ~96% mineral, translucent, n 1.63) over yellower dentin (n 1.54). Crowns are sex-dimorphic by 2-4% (canines most), set in an upper arch that overlaps the lower (overjet ~2 mm, overbite ~2 mm) and is wider. Clinical crowns lengthen with age (wear, recession); teeth darken and gain chroma with age to ~46 y (R1, R7, R14).
- Gingiva: coral pink, scalloped, papillae fill the embrasures; colour varies with ancestry and age by up to dE 5 (R18).
- Tongue: a muscular volume of ~70-90 mL, men ~30% larger than women; sagittal profile about 2.3 x longer than high; papillae roughen the dorsum (sizes not found). Palate: arch of 19-21 mm height; soft palate is a mobile flap.
- Opening: first ~20 mm of incisal separation is mostly condylar rotation, then the condyle also glides forward (R29); maximum 43-58 mm by population, ~0.3 mm per cm of stature. At rest the teeth are 2-4 mm apart (R29). Incisor show falls with age at rest (upper) and rises (lower); in a smile the upper lip rises ~80% of its length (R6, R27).
- Wet surfaces: a saliva film that is thick on tongue and floor (60-70 um), thin on the front palate (11 um) (R22).

## C. How to measure on the mesh
- Landmarks: ch (commissures), ls, li, sto, sn, sl, cphr/cphl (crista philtri), mouth corners at rest with teeth lightly apart. Distances straight 3-D, not projected (R2). Section: midsagittal for lip heights/angle; trace the vermilion border as a closed curve and report its length and the red-lip area; lip volume as sn/sto/ls tetrahedra (R2) or closed-mesh volume.
- Teeth: MD = widest distance between contact points along the arch, BL = widest perpendicular, crown height from gingival zenith to incisal edge along the long axis. State the arch landmark for every width: canine tip, mesiobuccal cusp tip and central fossa differ by 15 mm in the same arch (R7 vs R9). Overjet = horizontal distance between the labial upper and lower central incisal edges; overbite = vertical overlap.
- Arch / palate: arch depth = midline distance, labial central incisors to mesial first molars; palatal height as D11; sagittal palate section for curvature. Gingiva: zenith heights and papilla tips on the labial strip; sample L*a*b* on the buccal keratinised band 6 mm wide (R18).
- Tongue: closed-mesh volume at rest; midsagittal section: length tip to vallecula, height perpendicular to it, area; sample papilla relief on a 5 x 5 mm patch.
- Opening: incisal-edge distance (add overbite to the vertical gap, R29); mandible rotation = angle of the lower arch about the condylar axis; condyle path length from the mesh pair open/closed. Wetness: shell offset 10-70 um by site (D19).
