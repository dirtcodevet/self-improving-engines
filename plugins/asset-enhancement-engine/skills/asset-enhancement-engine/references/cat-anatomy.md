# Cat anatomy reference (domestic shorthair) - what the photo cannot show

Compiled 2026-10-17 by a read-only research agent (sources cited per line; "(snippet)" = search-result text only;
small samples - treat numbers as ranges). Used by the review (judging "natural") and by the face/junction specs.

## Head
- Skull length 86-99 mm; zygomatic (cheek-arch) width 61-69 mm; brain case only ~43 mm wide (PMC8402625,
  PMC13359505, Europe PMC PMC12784816). The 9-13 mm deep temporal fossa on each side is FILLED by the thick temporalis
  muscle (IMAIOS temporalis) -> from the front the line cheek -> ear base is straight or convex, NEVER concave; the
  top between the ears is a broad gentle arc ("no dome between ears", CFA American Shorthair standard).
  => our "indent above the cheeks / pointy top" = a bare-skull head without temporalis.
- Front view: ear base -> eye centres = eye centres -> chin tip (CFA). Head an oblong slightly longer than wide, full
  cheeks; nose medium length, same width throughout; gentle concave stop in profile; chin forms a perpendicular line
  with the upper lip in profile (CFA).
- Ears: medium, slightly rounded tips, not unduly open at the base; inner ear corners twice the eye spacing apart
  (CFA); cupped pinna with Henry's pocket at the lower back edge.

## Eyes
- Upper lid = half an almond (cut lengthwise), lower lid fully rounded; outer corners very slightly higher than inner
  (CFA). At least one eye-width between the eyes. No eyelashes. Third eyelid pale pink at the inner corner, hidden
  when alert. Palpebral fissure 20-28 mm (eurekamag 002451588; Wiley vop.13051); cornea ~16.5 x 16.2 mm (Carrington
  1986); globe ~21 mm; orbit ~24-28 mm. The cornea nearly fills the opening: almost no white shows.

## Nose
- Nasal planum: hairless, pigmented, bulging around the nostrils esp. dorsally (IMAIOS nasal plane); "ship anchor" in
  lynx: broad upper part around the nostrils + a stem down the upper-lip midline split by the philtrum.
- Surface: polygonal "cobblestone" cells separated by grooves (unique per cat); epidermis ~900 um, no glands
  (Strickland 1958); healthy = cobblestone, disease flattens it (Royal Canin). Cell size in cats not published -
  estimate sub-mm to ~1 mm: normal/displacement detail, not geometry. Less glossy than a dog's.
- Nostrils: carnivore slit convex ventrally, the lateral narrow end raised into a horizontal comma running into a
  barely marked alar groove (IMAIOS nostril); cat nostrils "semicircular" (Ain Shams). The alar fold sits just inside
  the opening -> the visible dark is a SHALLOW crescent, not a deep hole. Philtrum: shallow groove.

## Mouth (closed)
- Upper lip clearly larger than the lower (carnivores) and split by the philtrum into two lobes -> overhang.
- Whisker pads: ~12 whiskers per side in 4 rows; infraorbital foramina 27.7 +- 1.9 mm apart (pad width = that + soft
  tissue). Lip margins pigmented (lentigo sites). Corner (commissure) position and canthus angles: not found.

## Body junctions
- Neck: cervical spine held near VERTICAL at rest; the visible neck is a continuous thick muscle mass throat ->
  sternum and occiput -> withers, no waist (Vidal et al. 1986). Shoulder blade has no bony joint (no knob).
- Chest: manubrium "spear shaped", projecting forward of the first ribs; thorax width/depth ~0.7-1.3 -> trunk section
  an upright oval with the back muscles.
- Forelegs: an elbow fold (armpit web) joins upper-arm skin to the sternum and chest wall; the leg appears to emerge
  at the elbow near the sternum line.
- Hind legs: a flank fold in front of the thigh (two skin layers with fat) to the groin and flank; loose skin between
  thigh and crus; a loose belly "primordial pouch" in front of the hind legs.
- Tail: slight slope from hip to tail base; tail heavy at the base and tapering; length ~ shoulder blades to tail base;
  tail muscles start on the lower back and sacrum -> the root flows out of the croup, no ridge.
- Size: shoulder height 25-27 cm; humerus ~10 cm, radius ~9.5 cm, femur ~11 cm, tibia ~11.5 cm (PMC11598937).
